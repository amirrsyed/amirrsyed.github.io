Projects

EnergEyes.org

In 2022, I founded EnergEyes.org, a B2B service dedicated to achieving carbon neutrality through enterprise Net Zero investment, as a way to demonstrate how target markets with theoretical economic "perfect information" enable market forces to serve collective interests better than state regulation can. Customers seek profit gains by investing in and marketing the Net Zero carbon emissions status using our proprietary willingness to pay data and tokenized carbon credit platform. Cofounded with Wharton summer program students, EnergEyes.org improves environmental technology and maintains enterprise partnerships. The company provides (1) LLM-based strategic Net Zero investment calculation software and (2) carbon emissions futures market data aggregation services.

EnergEyes.org ceased operations and transitioned into an open source tool in 2025 after all major futures exchanges successfully began trading carbon emissions futures.
